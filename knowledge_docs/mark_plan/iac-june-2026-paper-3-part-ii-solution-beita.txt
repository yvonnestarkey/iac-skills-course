1 Non-compliance with laws and regulations
1.1 Payment below minimum wage:
Illegal - The company pay its employees at rates below the legislated
minimum wages and therefore do not adhere to National Minimum Wage
Act.
Other ethical frameworks - Dino has also indicated it will only apply for
exemption if someone asks for it, which appears to be dishonest business
dealings and is unfair for employees, the employees will have less
disposable income to spend, which will have a negative impact on the
employees and their families.
1
1
1.2 Purchasing of pallets:
Theft - The company is purchasing pallets that could potentially be stolen
goods (other company’s name, price clearly not market-related) and may
not be in compliance with the Prevention and Combating of Corrupt
Activities Act.
1
1.3 Appointment of auditors s90 Co Act:
The directors appointed auditors who also perform management
functions,such as tax returns and compilation of financial statements,
resulting in a
breach of s90(2) of the Companies Act.
1
1.4 Payment of dividends Co Act:
It appears that the requirements of the Companies Act to perform a
liquidity
test have not been performed as Beita is paying dividends when they
areonly performing a solvency test and no liquidity test.
1
1.5 Transfer of assets to Beita Furniture:
The company acquired the assets of an NPO. Assets of an NPO may only
be used for the purposes / objective for which it was set up and upon
dissolution should go to a similar NPO.
In addition, the assets were transferred at net book value and not at its
fair market value.
Transferring those assets at net book value into a for-profit company for
the private benefit of Dino and Siphiwe potentially breaches the conditions
of the public benefit organization approval in terms of the Income Tax Act
and the dissolution rule that such assets should pass to a similar
organization.
