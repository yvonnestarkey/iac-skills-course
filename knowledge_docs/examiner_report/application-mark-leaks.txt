Examiner commentary sample — application vs knowledge

Students often believe they failed because they did not know enough theory. Scripts show the opposite pattern: basic knowledge is present, but it is not converted.

Where application marks are lost:
- Discussion points are headings, not arguments.
- The required verb (discuss, advise, evaluate) is ignored.
- Facts from the case are not linked to the principle.
- Time is used on one sub-part while later required items are blank.

Coaching implication: calculate the BMCR, then practise articulation. Do not default to more revision until the conversion leak is closed.
